When it shows

Apps, in order

    Settings always stays: it opens the TV's full Settings page. Everything else you can add, remove or move.

    travel laptop · its own sections

    Shown on the travel laptop

    Waiting on you lists the questions your Claude sessions on the desk laptop are waiting on, newest first. Away from home it shows the last copy and says how old it is.

    Waiting on you sits

    The NEA two-hour forecast for one area, read every 10 minutes.

    In the travel laptop's dock

    Readings in the right-hand island, between the Claude block and CPU/GPU/RAM; Next event is the tile at its end. Music island is the left-hand island while something plays (the floating card stays hidden while it shows). Battery goes red at 20% or less unplugged; Away means the hub is out of reach and the wallpaper shows its last copy. If the dock runs out of room, CPU/GPU/RAM goes first.

    Only the travel laptop changes. The TV and the desk laptop keep the buses. Changing these switches the preview to the travel laptop.

    desk laptop · its dock

    In the desk laptop's dock

    Disk turns amber under 100 GB free; GPU at 75° and memory commit at 75% turn amber, red at 85. Memory commit shows once the hub reports it. Changing these switches the preview to the desk laptop.

    Add a wallpaper

    Upload any image you like. Claude cuts out its subject for the depth effect, picks a clock face and adds it to the rotation, then it shows up in the list above.

    Drop an image here, or

    JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 MB. Landscape works best: it is cropped to 16:9.

    The note goes with the next image you add. You can change it afterwards.
